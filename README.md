### ✦ about

I'm drawn to the space between an idea and the thing it becomes.
Most of what I make starts as a quiet *what if* — then I keep building
until it stops being a thought and starts being something you can use, explore, or get lost in.

<br/>

### ✦ what I believe

> **A good idea outlives its stack.**
> Tools change. The question you were trying to answer doesn't.

> **Complexity belongs underneath.**
> From the outside, things should feel simple — even when they aren't.

> **Unfinished just means alive.**
> The best ideas are still being discovered while you build them.
